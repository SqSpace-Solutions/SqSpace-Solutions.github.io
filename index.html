About Us

Welcome to SqSpace Solutions LLC, a scientific consulting practice dedicated to helping researchers, biotechnology organizations, and life science teams strengthen scientific quality, improve communication, and accelerate project success. Led by a biology-trained researcher with experience across academic, government, nonprofit, and industry laboratories, we provide expert support in scientific strategy, technical communication, research evaluation, and project management.

Our Services

Scientific Manuscript Editing & Experimental Strategy Feedback

Strengthen the scientific quality, clarity, and impact of your research. I provide in-depth scientific editing for manuscripts, grant applications, technical reports, and research documents. Beyond language refinement, I evaluate experimental design, data interpretation, scientific rigor, and logical consistency to help ensure that conclusions are fully supported by the evidence presented.

Services include:

• Manuscript and technical document editing

• Experimental design and methodology review

• Data interpretation and scientific logic assessment

• Hypothesis development and research narrative refinement

• Feedback on controls, experimental gaps, and future directions

• Scientific rigor, transparency, and reproducibility review

My approach preserves the author's scientific voice while helping strengthen the overall credibility and impact of the work.

Scientific Project Manager

Build efficient, scalable research environments that support innovation and growth. I help biotechnology startups, research organizations, and scientific teams transform complex scientific objectives into structured operational workflows. My focus is on improving research efficiency, reproducibility, and long-term scalability through strategic planning and laboratory operations support. Services include: • Laboratory workflow and process design • Sample management architecture and tracking systems • Equipment, resource, and infrastructure planning • Cross-functional team coordination • Biosafety-aligned operational strategies • Research planning, documentation, and progress reporting • Operational optimization for growing organizations The goal is to reduce operational friction, improve execution, and create systems that support successful translation from early-stage research to production readiness.

Scientific Content Creation & LLM Training

Transform complex scientific information into accurate, actionable, and accessible knowledge. I support researchers, biotechnology companies, and AI organizations through scientific content development, data interpretation, and life-science-focused AI training initiatives. Services include: • Publication-quality scientific figures and data visualization • Pathway analysis and biological dataset interpretation • Scientific communication for technical and multidisciplinary audiences • Development and review of life science content for AI systems • Annotation and validation of biological and biomedical datasets • Evaluation of model-generated scientific outputs • Scientific accuracy, fact-checking, and quality assurance By combining expertise in biology, scientific communication, and data analysis, I help ensure that scientific content and AI training materials are accurate, well-structured, and contextually meaningful for research, clinical, and translational applications.
